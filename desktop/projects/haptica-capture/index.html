Haptica Capture Rig

TRANSCRIBING DEXTERITY

Egocentric multimodal capture for robot skill learning

The System

Thesis

Robots will learn high-value manual skills from human demonstrations — and the data they need (what hands see, feel, and do) cannot be scraped from the internet. At Haptica I built the capture hardware to record it: synchronized vision, depth, tactile, and kinematic streams from skilled operators doing real work.

The backpack rig - Jetson AGX Orin in the pack, camera harness worn on the head.

Vision rig

  • Four global-shutter cameras (Stereolabs ZED X One GS over GMSL) on a Jetson AGX Orin: two form a calibrated stereo head pair producing live neural depth; two are wrist-mounted, looking down at the hands.
  • Frame-level simultaneity via the capture carrier's hardware master/slave sync, with an external-trigger mode for when a robot controller is the timing authority.
  • Clock discipline: wired PTP for multi-host rigs and a GNSS receiver for absolute UTC — the operator HUD shows a SYNC LOCKED indicator and UTC to the millisecond.

Tactile skin

Grip force is recorded on the same session clock as the video, by a custom flexible-PCB tactile sensor: a piezoresistive sheet over a printed interdigitated electrode matrix on flex, read out by a custom acquisition board.

Data pipeline

Sessions record as auto-rolling timestamped bundles (stereo + wrist video, depth preview, telemetry), stream over a 10 GbE ingest path — a raw-TCP transfer tool written after rsync-over-SSH plateaued around 230 MB/s — into a two-tier SSD→HDD archive with manifests, and export to robotics-standard formats (rosbag2/MCAP) plus a lightweight LeRobot package for training. A design principle throughout: preserve distinct clocks rather than flattening them.

Code

The public pieces of the capture stack: hapticajetson - Jetson AGX Orin bring-up for the ZED Link Quad carrier; phonecapture - Android UVC-to-Mac capture appliance; lifelog-compression and lifelog-audio-compression - Rust codecs for sparse visual and speech-first audio archives; more at github.com/hapticasensorics.

Status

The rig and tactile skin are working prototypes; Haptica's thesis has since evolved toward durable machine memory, with the capture program as its hardware foundation.
